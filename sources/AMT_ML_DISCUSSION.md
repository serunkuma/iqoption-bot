Integrating Auction Market Theory (AMT) into a machine learning pipeline shifts the model from guessing raw price patterns to identifying market balance, imbalance, and price acceptance vs. rejection.
However, there is a critical data constraint to solve first: standard OTC binary option feeds only give you price and timestamp—they lack true traded volume and Level 2 depth. To enforce AMT assumptions, you must reconstruct synthetic auction metrics using tick frequency (Time-Price Opportunity / TPO proxy) and build explicit AMT constraints into both your features and loss function.

1. Translating Auction Market Theory into Tick-Level Features

Since you do not have real order book volume in an OTC feed, you use Tick Density (Time Spent at Price) as your proxy for volume, following classic Market Profile (TPO) mechanics.
• Rolling Value Area (VAH, VAL, and POC). Group recent ticks (e.g., over a rolling 15-minute or 30-minute window) into discrete price bins (e.g., 0.5-pip increments). Identify the Point of Control (POC) as the price bin with the highest tick count, and compute the Value Area High (VAH) and Value Area Low (VAL) capturing 70% of all ticks in that window.
• Normalized Distance to Value. Feed the model signed distances from the current price \(P_{t}\) to the POC, VAH, and VAL, normalized by rolling volatility (σ):
\(Z_{\text{POC}}=\frac{P_{t}-\text{POC}}{\sigma },\quad Z_{\text{VAH}}=\frac{P_{t}-\text{VAH}}{\sigma },\quad Z_{\text{VAL}}=\frac{P_{t}-\text{VAL}}{\sigma }\)
• Excess & Single Prints (Rejection Tails). Measure the ratio of single-tick price levels at the extremes of a swing compared to the dense clusters inside the Value Area. Fast price spikes with very low tick counts indicate excess (rejection), whereas slow, high-tick-count grinding indicates acceptance.
• Initiative vs. Responsive Activity. Track tick arrival speed (Δ t) as price crosses VAH or VAL. Rapid tick acceleration breaking out of Value signals Initiative Buying/Selling (trend continuation), while decelerating ticks at the boundary signal Responsive Activity (mean reversion back to POC).

2. How to Make Predictions "In-Line" with AMT

Simply feeding AMT features into a neural network or tree model does not guarantee its predictions will obey AMT logic. You must enforce alignment through Regime Gating and Custom Loss Penalties.

Approach A: Two-Stage Regime Architecture (Recommended)

Split the decision into two explicit AMT states before predicting 1-minute direction:
1. Stage 1 — Market State Classifier (Balance vs. Imbalance): Classify whether the market is currently Rotational (Balanced) inside [VAL, VAH] or Directional (Imbalanced) breaking out with high tick velocity.
2. Stage 2 — Conditional Directional Models:
	• Balance Model (Mean-Reversion): Trained strictly on samples inside or slightly probing Value Area edges; heavily weighted to predict Puts near VAH and Calls near VAL targeting the POC.
	• Imbalance Model (Breakout Continuation): Trained strictly on samples where price breaks outside Value with high initiative tick speed; aligned with the breakout direction.

Approach B: AMT-Penalized Custom Loss Function

If using a single gradient-boosted model (LightGBM/XGBoost) or PyTorch neural network, add an AMT Structural Penalty to your binary cross-entropy loss:
\(\mathcal{L}_{\text{total}}=\mathcal{L}_{\text{BCE}}(y,\hat{p})+\lambda \cdot \text{Penalty}_{\text{AMT}}(\hat{p},Z_{\text{VAH}},Z_{\text{VAL}},\text{Velocity})\)
• If price is at VAH with low tick velocity (rejection setup) and the model outputs a high probability for a Call (p̂ > 0.5), the penalty term increases the loss.
• This forces the model's decision boundary to respect Value Area boundaries rather than chasing noisy micro-ticks.

3. Practical Reality Check on OTC vs. Real Auction Markets

AMT Component	Real Exchange (Futures / Spot ECN)	OTC Binary Options Feed
Price Discovery	Driven by real buyers and sellers negotiating fair value.	Generated by a broker's internal pricing algorithm.
Volume / POC	True contracts/lots traded at each price level.	Approximated via tick count (TPO proxy) only.
Liquidity Gaps	Real thin order books cause genuine price jumps.	Artificial jumps or flat-lines injected by server logic.
Edge Sustainability	High; institutional algorithms genuinely trade around VAH/VAL/POC.	Fragile; broker engines do not have real inventory to balance.

Key Takeaway: Because OTC feeds lack real institutional participants defending Value Areas, an AMT model on OTC data is essentially testing whether the broker's synthetic algorithm mimics real market microstructure. Often, retail OTC engines mirror live spot FX feeds during weekdays and switch to synthetic random-walk generators on weekends.
